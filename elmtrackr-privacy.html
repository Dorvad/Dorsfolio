2. Information We Collect

ElmTrackr collects information needed to provide its work-tracking, shift-reporting, payroll-estimation, and travel-refund features.

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, ElmTrackr collects and processes account information such as:

  • Email address
  • Authentication status
  • User ID or account identifier
  • Session information required to keep you signed in

Authentication is handled through Supabase. Passwords are processed by the authentication provider and are not stored by ElmTrackr in plain text.

Profile Information

You may choose to provide a display name or nickname. This is used to personalize the app experience, such as showing a greeting on the home screen.

Profile information may include:

  • Email address
  • Display name or nickname
  • Account creation and update timestamps

Shift and Work Records

ElmTrackr stores the work records you create in the app, including:

  • Shift start times
  • Shift end times
  • Break duration
  • Shift notes, if entered
  • Special-day, holiday, or weekend indicators
  • Refund-related shift status
  • Record creation and update timestamps

This information is used to provide the core shift-tracking functionality of the app.

Settings and Payroll Preferences

ElmTrackr stores settings and preferences that you configure, including:

  • Timezone or country preference
  • Daily overtime threshold
  • Weekly overtime threshold
  • Weekend-day preferences
  • Hourly base rate, if entered
  • Enabled or disabled app features
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Clock style and appearance preferences

Hourly rate and payroll settings are used only to calculate estimated compensation for personal tracking.

Payroll and Compensation Estimates

ElmTrackr may calculate estimated gross pay, overtime, holiday rates, travel refunds, or other compensation-related amounts based on information entered by the user.

These calculations are provided for personal tracking and convenience only. They are estimates and may not reflect the final amount paid by an employer, client, payroll provider, or other organization.

Actual compensation may be affected by taxes, social security contributions, pension deductions, insurance deductions, employer policies, collective agreements, local labor laws, payroll rounding rules, reimbursement policies, and other applicable regulations.

ElmTrackr does not provide legal, tax, payroll, accounting, or financial advice. Users should verify all compensation, tax, and reimbursement matters with their employer, accountant, payroll provider, or another qualified professional.

Travel Refund Data

If you enable or use travel-refund features, ElmTrackr may collect refund-related information, including:

  • Ride direction, such as to work or from work
  • Travel provider, such as Lime, Dott, Bird, Taxi, or Other
  • Ride date and time
  • Refund amount
  • Refund notes, if entered
  • Claim status
  • Related shift information

This information is used to help you track, review, and export travel refund claims.

Receipt Uploads

You may optionally upload receipt photos for travel refund claims.

Receipt files may contain information visible in the image, such as:

  • Vendor or provider name
  • Travel provider
  • Date and time
  • Ride or transaction details
  • Payment references
  • Partial payment information
  • Other information included on the receipt

Receipt files are stored using Supabase Storage and are used to help you manage and export refund claims.

Reports, Analytics, and Exports

ElmTrackr uses your saved shift, settings, and refund data to generate summaries, analytics, CSV exports, and PDF refund reports.

Reports and exports are created at your request and are intended for your personal use. If you share exported files with an employer, accountant, payroll provider, or any other third party, you are responsible for that sharing.

Local Device Storage

ElmTrackr may store certain preferences locally on your device or browser to improve performance and preserve your preferences.

This may include:

  • Cached settings
  • Theme or appearance preferences
  • Temporary interface state

Local storage helps the app load faster and keep your preferred settings available.

Technical Information

When you use ElmTrackr, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the app or by third-party service providers used to operate it. This may include:

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address
  • Authentication logs
  • App usage logs
  • Error logs
  • Basic security and performance information

This information is used to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the app.
